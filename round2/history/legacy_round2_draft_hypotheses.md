|---|---|

| `R2-draft/H16` | Heterogeneous integration scales better through a semantic layer while preserving entity-resolution correctness. |
| `R2-draft/H17` | Ontology reduces agent task-specific tooling burden for unseen tasks. |
| `R2-draft/H18` | Novel cross-domain discovery/query is cheaper with ontology. |
| `R2-draft/H19` | Novel policy composition is cheaper with ontology. |
| `R2-draft/H20` | Semantic mapping isolates source-schema churn and reduces downstream blast radius. |
| `R2-draft/H21` | Relative ontology advantage should increase with semantic complexity if a real semantic advantage exists. |
| `R2-draft/H22` | Adaptability gains must outweigh recurring ontology-specific operational tax in at least one realistic stable region. |

Their contracts are not normative for Round 2 v2. The new H15–H22 contracts in `/hypotheses/` are normative after protocol freeze.
