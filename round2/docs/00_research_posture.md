# 00 — Research posture

Round 2 v2 explicitly moves from ontology construction to **ontology hypothesis testing**.

The central attractor is:

> Discover the minimal architecture of an operational ontology, then build an open-source implementation of it.

The project is therefore allowed to discover that some proposed layer is unnecessary.

## Current adversarial stance

The strongest fact entering this round is the rejection of the bounded-domain comparative thesis in `exp-003`. That result prevents the project from treating semantic sophistication itself as value.

Round 2 v2 must answer:

1. What behavior actually requires an Ontology Language rather than conventional schemas/code?
2. Does a shared meta-model remain bounded as semantic variety increases?
3. Can Engine and Toolchain derive behavior from the language instead of hand-coded domain paths?
4. Does self-hosting expose real advantages or merely create a more complicated project tracker?
5. At what domain count, if any, does reuse repay ontology tax?

## No architecture by aesthetic attraction

"Operational elegance" is desirable only after correctness of the hypothesis is established. Structural beauty, recursion, self-description or a compact language are not evidence by themselves.
