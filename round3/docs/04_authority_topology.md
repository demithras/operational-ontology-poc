# 04 — Authority topology and the procedural/content boundary

Round 3 distinguishes two questions:

1. **Authority validity:** who may decide, within what scope, with what precedence/delegation/review/quorum/emergency rules?
2. **Discretionary content judgment:** what should the authorized decider conclude on a technically or socially underdetermined question?

H25 claims the first can be generically executable. It explicitly does **not** claim that a generic Engine can reconstruct every discretionary merit judgment.

A human, technical evaluator, consensus process or external oracle may supply a content judgment as an attributable fact. The Engine may verify that the fact came from a legitimate authority and was in scope; it must not manufacture the judgment and then call that authority reasoning.
